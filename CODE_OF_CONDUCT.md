# Code of Conduct

[简体中文](CODE_OF_CONDUCT.zh-CN.md)

CalcKernel is a technical project where people with different backgrounds
should be able to learn, contribute, and discuss ideas safely and respectfully.
This policy applies to project repositories, issues, pull requests, discussions,
documentation, and other spaces where someone is representing CalcKernel.

## Expected behavior

- Treat people with respect and patience; critique ideas and code without
  attacking the people behind them.
- Make room for different experience levels, communication styles, and
  perspectives.
- Keep feedback specific, constructive, and relevant to the project.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish another person's private information,
  credentials, or private communications without permission.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, discrimination, threats, sexualized attention, personal attacks,
deliberate intimidation, doxxing, and repeated disruptive conduct are not
acceptable. This includes behavior through project channels and targeted
conduct outside them that creates a hostile project environment.

## Maintainer response

Maintainers may edit or remove harmful content and may issue a warning, limit
participation, or remove someone from project spaces. Responses should be
proportionate to the behavior and may be immediate when needed to protect
participants. Maintainers should explain moderation decisions when doing so
does not increase harm or expose private information.

To report abusive content hosted on GitHub, use GitHub's [reporting tools](https://docs.github.com/en/communities/maintaining-your-safety-on-github/reporting-abuse-or-spam).
Do not post another person's private information in a public issue. For a
security vulnerability, use the private process in [`SECURITY.md`](SECURITY.md).

This is an original CalcKernel policy, informed by common open-source community
standards. It is not a legal agreement and does not replace GitHub's policies.
